## Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the site and improve its content.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, browser and device information, approximate location, and online identifiers.

Analytics is optional and is only enabled if you accept analytics cookies through our consent banner. You can reject analytics or change your preference at any time using the **Cookie settings** control on the privacy policy page.

We use this information to understand website usage. We do not intend to use Google Analytics to identify individual visitors directly.

Google may process this information on our behalf and, depending on the service configuration, in other countries. For more information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Cookies and Consent

We store your analytics preference in your browser so that we can remember your choice on subsequent visits.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Deleting cookies does not remove information already collected by Google Analytics.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For more information about your rights in the UK, visit the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/).
