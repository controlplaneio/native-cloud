---
title: Ease of Adoption
description: Best practices and advice for native.cloud keys.
---

Adopting `native.cloud` doesn't have to mean more work. Think of it like the [12 Factor App](https://12factor.net/) principles — guidance, not bureaucracy. You can adopt it gradually, selectively, and in ways that fit your workflow:

- **As a reference.** When designing a new service or resource, consult the keys that apply. No process change required — just a better starting point.
- **TODO Questionnaire CLI.** The questionnaire CLI can guide you through the ontology and quickly identify the keys you'd benefit from the most. It has two running modes: raw questions and the LLM backed conversational approach.
- **TODO AI skills.** If you'd rather utilize your existing AI harness there is a skill to help understand your requirements and surface suggestions like the LLM backed conversational approach of the questionnaire CLI. Additionally there's a skill for general ontology lookup to provide context and the rules behind each key.
